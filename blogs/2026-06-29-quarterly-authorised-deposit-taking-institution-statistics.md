Breadcrumb Home Statistical Publication Quarterly authorised deposit-taking institution statistics Banking Published 29 June 2026 Print On this page On this page APRA publishes statistics on authorised deposit-taking institutions (ADIs) on a quarterly basis. The industry performance statistics contains aggregate data on the financial performance and position, capital adequacy, asset quality, liquidity and key performance ratios of ADIs. This is supplemented by entity level statistics for key capital and liquidity data.
